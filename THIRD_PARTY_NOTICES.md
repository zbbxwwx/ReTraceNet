# Third-party components

## CAT-Net DCT branch

The DCT cue encoder in `retracenet/compression_dct_encoder.py` is adapted from the DCT branch of CAT-Net, and its optional double-JPEG initialization originates from CAT-Net pretrained weights. Those weights are not distributed in this repository.

When using or redistributing this component, review the upstream project license and cite:

> Myung-Joon Kwon, In-Jae Yu, Seung-Hun Nam, and Heung-Kyu Lee. CAT-Net: Compression Artifact Tracing Network for Detection and Localization of Image Splicing. WACV 2021.
